# Associate Cloud Engineer Exam-Day Guide

This page covers the logistics of the Google Cloud Associate Cloud Engineer exam: how to book it, how the two delivery options differ, what to have ready, how the exam is built and scored, and what happens after you finish. Every fact below links to the official Google Cloud page it came from. Google updates its policies from time to time, so confirm anything time-sensitive on the linked page before your appointment.

> Also see the [Associate Cloud Engineer practice questions](https://www.savemycert.com/practice/gcp-associate-cloud-engineer/?utm_source=github&utm_medium=readme&utm_campaign=gcp-associate-cloud-engineer-study-guide) and [timed mock exams](https://www.savemycert.com/mocks/gcp-associate-cloud-engineer/?utm_source=github&utm_medium=readme&utm_campaign=gcp-associate-cloud-engineer-study-guide).

## Booking the exam

Google Cloud exams are delivered by Pearson VUE. Google moved from Kryterion (Webassessor) to Pearson in early 2026, so older guides that mention Webassessor are out of date. You still register through Google's CM Connect portal: choose **Register** on the exam page, create a CM Connect account if you're new, then choose **Schedule/Launch an Exam**. You pick the delivery method and language, then CM Connect signs you in to Pearson to choose a date, time, and location.

The standard exam costs $125 plus tax where applicable, the same for online and onsite delivery. It is offered in English, Japanese, Spanish, and Portuguese. There are no prerequisites, but you must be 18 or older. Google recommends 6+ months of hands-on Google Cloud experience.

Your legal first and last name in CM Connect must match your government-issued photo ID, in Romanized characters. If they don't match, you can't test and you lose the fee.

Reschedule or cancel from CM Connect at least 24 hours before your start time. Later changes carry a late fee, and a no-show forfeits the fee with no refund.

Source: [Associate Cloud Engineer](https://cloud.google.com/learn/certification/cloud-engineer), [Upcoming Change to Exam Delivery Provider](https://support.google.com/cloud-certification/answer/16803278), [Registration and Scheduling](https://support.google.com/cloud-certification/answer/9907651), [Google Cloud Certification FAQs](https://support.google.com/cloud-certification/answer/9438208), [Important Program Policies](https://support.google.com/cloud-certification/answer/10220719)

## Online proctored vs test center

You can test online through Pearson's OnVUE service or onsite at a Pearson test center. The rules differ in ways that matter:

- **Test center:** arrive at least 15 minutes early with two forms of ID. Personal items go in a locker, and no food or drink is allowed.
- **Online proctored:** run Pearson's system test and download OnVUE at least 24 hours before the exam. Use a quiet, well-lit room with no one else in it, a clear desk, and external monitors disconnected. No food, drink, or headphones. Pearson strongly recommends a personal computer over a corporate one. Check-in is automated: you photograph yourself and your ID and do a 360-degree room scan.
- **No breaks online.** Google's online exams allow no breaks without an approved accommodation. Taking one cancels the session and Google rejects the result. Leaving the camera's view or talking aloud can also end it.

Google's help page says you can launch an online exam from 10 minutes before to 20 minutes after your scheduled time. Miss that window and the session is canceled and the fee forfeited.

Source: [Testing requirements for proctored exams](https://support.google.com/cloud-certification/answer/9907852), [Online Proctored Exams](https://support.google.com/cloud-certification/answer/9907748), [Upcoming Change to Exam Delivery Provider](https://support.google.com/cloud-certification/answer/16803278)

## What to have ready

**ID.** Online, you need a government-issued photo ID. At a test center, bring two forms of ID, one of them a government-issued photo ID. The secondary ID needs your name and signature, such as a credit card.

**Time.** Your appointment includes an extra 15 minutes for check-in, agreements, and surveys.

Source: [Acceptable forms of identification](https://support.google.com/cloud-certification/answer/9907450), [Registration and Scheduling](https://support.google.com/cloud-certification/answer/9907651)

## How the exam is structured

You get 2 hours for 50 to 60 multiple-choice and multiple-select questions. The exam is closed book: no notes or other materials, online or onsite.

The exam guide has four sections: setting up a cloud solution environment (~20%), planning and implementing a cloud solution (~30%), ensuring the successful operation of a cloud solution (~30%), and configuring access and security (~20%). Google publishes these as approximate percentages.

Source: [Associate Cloud Engineer](https://cloud.google.com/learn/certification/cloud-engineer), [Associate Cloud Engineer exam guide (PDF)](https://services.google.com/fh/files/misc/associate_cloud_engineer_exam_guide_english.pdf), [Online Proctored Exams](https://support.google.com/cloud-certification/answer/9907748)

## How scoring works

Results are pass or fail only. Google does not report a numerical score, because the exam only decides whether you meet a minimum standard. You don't need to pass each section, only the exam as a whole.

Source: [Google Cloud Certification FAQs](https://support.google.com/cloud-certification/answer/9438208)

## Results and your credential

You see a provisional pass or fail on screen as soon as you submit. Google then reviews your exam record, including compliance with the terms and conditions, so allow 7 to 10 days for official confirmation. If you fail, a report under Exam History breaks your performance down by section.

Google Cloud credentials live in a Credential Wallet powered by Credly, which emails you instructions to claim your badge and lets you share it on LinkedIn or a website.

Source: [Google Cloud Certification FAQs](https://support.google.com/cloud-certification/answer/9438208), [Your Google Cloud Credential Wallet](https://support.google.com/cloud-certification/answer/13258004)

## Validity and renewal

The certification is valid for 3 years. Associate renewal opens 180 days before it expires, and you have three options:

- **Shorter renewal exam:** 1 hour, 20 questions, $75 plus tax, in English and Japanese. Passing adds 3 years.
- **Standard exam:** retake the full exam during the renewal window.
- **Google Skills:** complete designated courses or skill badges in the last year of your certification. This adds 1 year.

Once you choose the shorter or standard exam path for ACE, you can't switch after your first attempt. You get a 50% renewal discount code in CM Connect when you first certify.

Source: [Associate Cloud Engineer](https://cloud.google.com/learn/certification/cloud-engineer), [Certification Renewal Options](https://support.google.com/cloud-certification/answer/9907853), [Google Cloud Certification FAQs](https://support.google.com/cloud-certification/answer/9438208)

## If you don't pass

- Wait 14 days before your second attempt.
- Wait 60 days before a third attempt, and 365 days before a fourth.
- You get at most four attempts in two years, across all languages and delivery methods.
- You pay each time you register.

Source: [Retake Policy](https://support.google.com/cloud-certification/answer/9749448)

## On exam day: practical tips

- **Pace yourself.** Up to 60 questions in 120 minutes is about 2 minutes each.
- **Settle in first.** With no breaks allowed online, handle water and restroom before you launch.
- **Read for the constraint.** Words like "least privilege," "most cost-effective," or "fewest steps" usually decide between two workable answers.
